1. "Draft the abstract for my paper from these results."
2. "Help me write the introduction and related work section."
3. "Polish my manuscript before the CVPR deadline."
4. "Suggest a title and acronym for my method."

## Must stay quiet
1. "Summarize this arXiv paper for me." → `read-paper`
2. "Survey the literature on diffusion models." → `research-workflow`
3. "Review my pull request." → not a paper task
